I am a methodological statistician from Islamabad, Pakistan. 

My passion for statistics started during my M.Sc., where I discovered the power of mathematical modeling. 

I am incredibly proud of my 7 publications, which I completed while serving full-time as a Constable in the Pakistan Railways Police. This experience taught me immense discipline and time management.

Outside of research, I enjoy teaching the next generation of statisticians as a Visiting Lecturer at PMAS-Arid Agriculture University.
