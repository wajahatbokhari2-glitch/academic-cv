I am a methodological statistician from Islamabad, Pakistan. 

My passion for statistics started during my M.Sc., where I discovered the power of mathematical modeling to solve real-world problems. 

I am incredibly proud of my **7 peer-reviewed publications**, which I completed while serving full-time as a Constable in the Pakistan Railways Police. This experience taught me immense discipline, resilience, and exceptional time management.

Outside of research, I enjoy teaching the next generation of statisticians as a Visiting Lecturer at PMAS-Arid Agriculture University, where I deliver graduate-level courses on Regression Analysis, Hypothesis Testing, and Nonparametric Statistics.
