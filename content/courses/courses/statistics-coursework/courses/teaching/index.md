## Visiting Lecturer, Department of Statistics (2025 - Present)

As a Visiting Lecturer at the Department of Statistics, PMAS-Arid Agriculture University, I deliver rigorous, graduate-level instruction and supervise student research. 

### Courses Taught:
- **Hypothesis Testing** – Theory and application of parametric and non-parametric testing procedures.
- **Regression Analysis** – Linear, multiple, and logistic regression with practical implementation in R.
- **Nonparametric Statistics** – Distribution-free inference, rank-based tests, and robustness.
- **Introduction to Statistical Inference** – Estimation theory, confidence intervals, and properties of estimators.
- **Applied Sampling Techniques** – Survey design, random sampling methods, and estimation of population parameters.

---

### Teaching Responsibilities:
- Delivering lectures and tutorials on undergraduate and graduate-level statistical methods.
- Supervising student projects and providing academic mentoring to foster analytical thinking and research skills.
- Developing and assessing course materials, examinations, and practical assignments.
- Contributing to departmental academic discussions and supporting curriculum development in applied statistics.
